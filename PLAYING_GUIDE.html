🎹 8 Iconic Studio Instruments

  • 🪗 Indian Harmonium: Authentic double and triple brass reeds, bellows wind, and drone notes.
  • 🎹 Concert Grand Piano & Rhodes: Felt hammer knock (<3ms), trichord unisons with micro-detuning (+0.8/-0.8 cents), soundboard resonance, multi-harmonic decay, and Rhodes tine ring.
  • 🪕 Classical Indian Sitar: Dynamic Jawari curved bridge buzz filter envelope (2.4-3.8 kHz), steel mizrab strike, and taraf sympathetic string resonance.
  • 🎼 Kashmiri Santoor: 100-string hammered dulcimer with walnut mezrab chime, natural 4-string chorus beating, and long crystalline sustain.
  • 🎷 Studio Jazz Saxophone: Cane reed vibration, conical brass bore acoustic resonance, breath turbulence noise, and 5.4 Hz delayed vibrato.
  • 🎻 Modern Concert Violin: Tactile rosin bow friction, 4-stage wooden corpus resonance (Helmholtz A0, top B1, back, and bridge hill), and delayed-onset vibrato.
  • 🪈 Real Bansuri Flute: Natural bamboo breath turbulence, authentic embouchure chiff, and expressive vibrato.
  • 🎸 Acoustic Guitar: Plucked string transient attack, soundboard wooden body decay, and crystal-clear sustain.
