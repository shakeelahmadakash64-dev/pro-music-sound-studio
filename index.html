  • 🪈 Real Bansuri Flute: Bamboo breath turbulence, embouchure chiff, and expressive vibrato.
  • 🎻 Modern Concert Violin: Tactile rosin friction, 4-stage body formants, and delayed vibrato.
  • 🎸 Acoustic Guitar: Plucked string strike with soundboard wood decay.
  • 🎹 Concert Grand Piano: Felt hammer knock, trichord string unisons, and soundboard sustain.
  • 🪕 Classical Indian Sitar: Dynamic jawari bridge buzz, steel mizrab attack, and taraf sympathetic ring.
  • 🎼 Kashmiri Santoor: 100-string hammered dulcimer with walnut mezrab chime and long sustain.
  • 🎷 Studio Saxophone: Cane reed vibration, conical brass bore, and smoky expressive breath.
