📖 Pro Music & Sound Studio 4.0 — Help Center & What's New

🎉 What's New in Version 4.0 (Pro Studio Edition)

Welcome to Pro Music & Sound Studio 4.0! This major upgrade transforms your virtual instrument into an all-in-one Digital Audio Workstation (DAW) designed for all musicians, screen reader users, and students:

  • 8 Iconic Acoustic Instruments: Expanded from 4 to 8 studio instruments with physical acoustic synthesis — Indian Harmonium, Concert Grand Piano & Rhodes, Classical Indian Sitar, Kashmiri Santoor, Studio Jazz Saxophone, Modern Concert Violin, Real Bansuri Flute, and Acoustic Guitar.
  • 1-Click Instrument Deck: Quick 8-card visual deck with instant 1-click selection, active glow state, and single-key cycling (F2 or I).
  • Dedicated Settings Menu (F4 / Alt + O): Full tabbed modal to configure Audio Engine, Screen Reader verbosity, Keyboard display, and Studio Themes.
  • Punjabi Wedding Dhol (1 to 8): Authentic sheesham wood drum with deep Dagga bass, sharp Tilli rim snap, rolls, and dhamal strokes.
  • Classical Tabla (16 Bols): Play authentic tabla bols on keys Z to /.
  • Collapsible Master Effects Rack (Alt + S): Reverb spaces, ping-pong delay, 3-band parametric EQ, and analog tube warmth.
  • Zero-Touch Automatic Updates: Background GitHub synchronization on startup without needing to run any manual batch files.
  • 100% Offline & Ad-Free: Pure Web Audio API synthesis with zero external dependencies, trackers, or advertisements.

🎛️ 8 Iconic Studio Instruments Guide

Press F2 or I to cycle forward through instruments, or press Shift + F2 to cycle backward:

  • 🪗 Indian Harmonium: Authentic single, double, and triple brass acoustic reeds, expressive bellows air dynamics, and drone keys.
  • 🎹 Concert Grand Piano & Rhodes: Physical hammer knock (<3ms), trichord unisons with micro-detuning (+0.8/-0.8 cents), soundboard resonance, multi-harmonic decay, and Rhodes tine ring.
  • 🪕 Classical Indian Sitar: Dynamic Jawari curved bridge buzz (2.4-3.8 kHz formant envelope), steel mizrab stroke, and taraf sympathetic string resonance.
  • 🎼 Kashmiri Santoor: 100-string hammered dulcimer with walnut mezrab chime, natural 4-string chorus beating, and long crystalline sustain.
  • 🎷 Studio Jazz Saxophone: Cane reed vibration, conical brass bore acoustic resonance, breath turbulence noise, and 5.4 Hz delayed vibrato.
  • 🎻 Modern Concert Violin: Tactile rosin bow friction, 4-stage wooden corpus resonance (Helmholtz A0, top B1, back, and bridge hill), and delayed-onset vibrato.
  • 🪈 Real Bansuri Flute: Natural bamboo breath turbulence, authentic embouchure chiff, and expressive vibrato.
  • 🎸 Acoustic Guitar: Plucked string transient attack, soundboard wooden body decay, and crystal-clear sustain.

🔄 Automatic Updates & GitHub Synchronization

Pro Music & Sound Studio 4.0 is connected to GitHub (shakeelahmadakash64-dev/pro-music-sound-studio) for zero-maintenance updates:

  • Zero-Touch Auto-Update: When launched from your Desktop shortcut, the studio automatically checks and pulls any new updates from GitHub silently in the background. You do not need to run any manual files.
  • In-App Update Check: Every time the studio opens, it checks GitHub in the background. If a newer version is available, a "What's New" notification banner appears with full details.
  • 100% Offline Resilience: If your computer has no internet connection, the studio skips the check and runs instantly from offline storage.

♿ Screen Reader & Accessibility Guide (NVDA & JAWS)

  • Automatic Focus Mode: The studio operates under role="application", meaning NVDA and JAWS automatically switch to focus mode so every keystroke passes directly to your instruments with zero latency.
  • Live ARIA Announcements: All instrument switches, percussion bank changes, settings adjustments, and update alerts are announced through polite and assertive ARIA live regions.
  • Zero Key Collision: You can play melody keys (A to \) with your right hand while simultaneously playing dhol/drums (1 to 8) or tabla (Z to /) with your left hand without any audio dropouts or key clashes.
  • Emergency Silence: Press Escape at any time to immediately mute all sounding voices.
